Marked by Water

1 Some days when you stop at the swim spot
2 instead of walking up to the bridge
3 you swim now while you can
4 because you know hot days lie ahead when the river will be low.
5
6 You believe the current is strong enough to wash away the past,
7 so you lay your head back to submerge your ears into the Earth
8 to hear the years as they break away and head down river
9 into another dimension.
10
11 Listening long enough to the fluid passing of time,
12 you loose your fingers gripping the riverbed
13 where water and time have created nostalgia, 
14 and you drift downstream.
15
16 That day the stream moved fast, 
17 yet submerged in water, summertime slowed 
18 long enough to enjoy the cool present,
19 aware of the past and reconciled to any future.
