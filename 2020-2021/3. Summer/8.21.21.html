Months Later

1 I’ve kept that bloody, long-eared hare alive 
2 in all the illusion destroyed and recreated;
3 I heard you retell the story like I wasn’t there,
4 but your words keep that rabbit alive,
5 even when they are buckeyed lies.
6 Truth is advertised.
7
8 The real and that in your mind are not the same.
9 Selling the product of power is always propaganda.
