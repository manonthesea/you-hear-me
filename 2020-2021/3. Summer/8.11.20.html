Madness At-Hand

1 Now madness hides in the virus,
2 but semblance was lost in the host.
3 A twitch so common, some call it locomotion.
4 A pounding so hard, it hides from where it comes.
5 The world so ajar, it hangs off its hinges.
6
7 It seems and seems, and the seeming is incessant. 
8 It seems Pilate is a well-liked man,
9 Judas turns a profit off his investment,
10 and the devil lives in a penthouse suite with an open door, 
11 where some dare to go. 
12
13 I put my face in my unwashed hands
14 with no regard for the novelty
15 of death and fear in our time 
16 without communion.
17 Ever-seeming, if a Savior ever was, he is gone, 
18 neither here nor in the now. 

8.11.20
