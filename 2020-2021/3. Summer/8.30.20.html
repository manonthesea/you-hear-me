Shooting Marbles

1 My father is Peter Pan on acid,
2 the youngest of the Merry Pranksters, 
3 tagging along the return caravan west to California, 
4 making detours, spending nights on communes,
5 drinking mescaline soup,
6 sitting with “turn on, tune in, and drop out” gurus.
7
8 My father is my father, 
9 and despite never growing older,
10 time still passes in Neverland.
11 History is still made, 
12 sins are still had, 
13 and his are now mine.
14
15 Perhaps I could be comfortable 
16 in his Pan shoes 
17 even when he wakes,
18 kicks his toe, and screams
19 off to the cold work waiting for him
20 day in and day out;
21 by the time you see him again
22 warmed by a sixer
23 and the ferry dust of his delusions,
24 my dad is now all grins.
25
26 Perhaps I can be the best of this boy-man,
27 and wear a smile for the lost boys, 
28 stuck in disbelief
29 that time ever existed 
30 before now
31 when all that matters
32 is shooting marbles.

8.30.20
