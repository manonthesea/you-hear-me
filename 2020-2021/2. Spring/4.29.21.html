Eat to Remember

1 After a late night,
2 sometime after death,
3 in the early, purple morning,
4 a friend makes breakfast over an open fire 
5 and repeatedly asks if you love them.
6
7 Having come to find us even after death, 
8 words over breakfast remind us
9 to tend to the sheep with love.
10 Break bread, partake of the cup, 
11 and remember Love is greatest of these.
12
13 Love is a meal prepared
14 for the people carrying a heavy yoke.
15 Unexplainable mysteries are no sustenance
16 like a savior serving breakfast at dusk.
17 Eat to remember.
