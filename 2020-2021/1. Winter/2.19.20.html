Your Song in the Morning

1 Your song in the morning wakes me 
2 and answers my silent question.
3
4 Over coffee, you speak with exaggeration 
5 and nose through your feed like a pigeon.
6
7 Midday, it is hard to hear your melody 
8 and not love you completely.
9
10 By the time our eyelids feel heavy, 
11 we could do without a sound,
12
13 but as we fall into sublimation, 
14 you whisper found comprehension.  

2.19.20
