Winter is Coming

1 Mother Mary, sing me a lullaby.
2 Hush the ringing in my ears. 
3 Feels like I am slowly dying,
4 so sing the one about yesteryears.
5 Turn back time to a day
6 when spring flowers grew high.
7
8 Sing the line about the rabbits that play.
9 I love the refrain, “the herons fly.
10 Here we stand, you and I.
11 Here we stand, you and I, as the heron flies.”

11.21.24
