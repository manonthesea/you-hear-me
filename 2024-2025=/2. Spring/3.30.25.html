A Morning Without You

1 It is another day, 
2 and I woke without you.
3 You will be coming home soon,
4 and I hope together we will lay.
5
6 I think of you in the light
7 of the early morning room. 
8 I look for you to be home soon
9 before the cold chill of the night.
10
11 You will be there to keep me warm
12 when our bed is lit by the moon.

14 because I have my lover in my arms.
