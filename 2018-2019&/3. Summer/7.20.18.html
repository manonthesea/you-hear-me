Looking Back After a Friend Dies

1 Praises go up 
2 for the little old black dog 
3 who sunbathes in the grass
4 as heat rises visibly alive.
5
6 Blessings come down
7 because our world aflame is loved
8 as that dog loves me, 
9 steady through fire.
10
11 Hands raise
12 in a benediction unknowing
13 yet prescient as time existing 
14 on the other side of yesterday.
