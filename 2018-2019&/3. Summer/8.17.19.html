Muddled Soup

1 The heat swells above the asphalt streets, 
2 and she lives dead in her breathing.
3 Subject to the oppressive swelter of summer,
4 her thoughts are neurotic and fumbled.
5
6 Not the only one on her back
7 upon the bed with a will that lacks 
8 from dawn to dusk, Texas is muddled soup. 
9 She thinks in nothing but dead-end loops.
10 Where a spark once set neurons ablaze
11 like dried-grass, her mind is confused, charred, and a waste.
