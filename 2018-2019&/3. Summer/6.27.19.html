The End of the Line

1 Just after the solstice, time seems to slip — 
2 like water over rock — a regular, modern blip, 
3 a stream that buffers and skips.
4
5 Clouds forever long formed 
6 mount into a generational storm 
7 upon a coast line ill-prepared to see morn.
8
9 With the sun no longer at its highest
10 and the steady rising rivers about to crest,
11 together let us work on what comes next.
12
13 Otherwise, time’s opposition to infinity 
14 and tomorrow’s tendency to become history
15 insist our extinction is likely.
