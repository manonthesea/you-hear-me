Emmanuel is Hard to See

1 At the card table Saturday night,
2 we sat as our spirits were somewhere else.
3 Winter neared, and we were caught
4 in the snowstorm of our conscience. 
5
6 Emmanuel is hard to see on the horizon, 
7 hidden by the ineffability of a blizzard,
8 not of the sky but of the mind.
9 Place and time fix as certainty spins.
10
11 The word is the form everlasting
12 of a storm that cannot be grasped.

12.11.18
