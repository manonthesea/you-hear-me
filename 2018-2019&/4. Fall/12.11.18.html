At the card table Saturday night,

1 we sat as our spirits were somewhere else.
2 Winter neared, and we were caught
3 in the snowstorm of our conscience. 
4
5 Emmanuel is hard to see on the horizon, 
6 hidden by the ineffability of a blizzard,
7 not of the sky but of the mind.
8 Place and time fix as certainty spins.
9
10 The word is the form everlasting
11 of a storm that cannot be grasped.
