Inside a Fish’s Belly

1 We slept outside on the open ground one summer last century. 
2 After a day advertised with fish jumping into our boats, 
3 no trout were taking the bait.
4 Almost a mile high, 
5 the sun’s heat pushed up just below the century mark.
6 I remember my white thighs burning red
7 atop that tin boat just staring at that mirrored glass water.   
8 I suspect it was the first day the planet started warming for good. 
9
10 Crane Prairie Reservoir ties time and memory together 
11 like an improved clinch knot: 
12 that season is a line threaded through the eye of my mind’s hook. 
13 When the sun is high and the air is thin, 
14 the burning white of my high thigh pulls on the line.
15 The snowy peak years later appears in my memories
16 like I am back harvesting dragonfly nymphs from beneath logs,
17 along the nearly algae-free shoreline.
18
19 Like a swallowed hook inside a fish’s belly,  
20 what is remembered hides a point about the time past. 
21 A nose under the stars, we laid our beds upon an anthill on a mountain
22 only a multitude of mosquitoes away from infinity.
23 The buzzing so intense
24 we took whatever was in Dad’s pocket to nod off.
25 As much as it took to fall asleep lakeside on that mountain,
26 the more it takes to turn back time and experience it again.

10.6.18
