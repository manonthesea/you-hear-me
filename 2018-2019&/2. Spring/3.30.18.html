Friday Irony

1 Their young bodies unsettled in the Good Friday service 
2 cause the pew to pray for faith and strength to tolerate 
3 the deteriorating mood of my youth
4 sent to play as the men mourn the slain son.
5
6 No piety exists in the screams 
7 raining down on the diminutive faces
8 of boys made to mourn slain men
9 from a foreign land with an indiscernible tongue
10 as the automobile moves away
11 from the holy rites of an unnerving Friday.

3.30.18
