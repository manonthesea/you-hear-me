Friday Irony

1 Their young bodies unsettle 
2 in the Good Friday service 
3 causing the last pew to pray 
4 for faith and strength 
5 to tolerate my youth 
6 before being sent to play 
7 as the old mourn the slain son.
8
9 No piety exists in the screams 
10 raining down on the diminutive faces
11 with sweet innocent cheeks and small ideas, 
12 boys made to mourn slain men
13 from a foreign land with an indiscernible tongue.
14
15 The automobile on the way home is a captive idea.
16 Why are holy rites unnerving Fridays?
