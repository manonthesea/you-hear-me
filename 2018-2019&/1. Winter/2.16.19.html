Desert Morning Chill

1 A swarm of moths freeze to stone 
2 surrounding the forgotten outside lamp.
3
4 Cold, night air covers over fading embers, 
5 and warm-blooded creatures retire 
6
7 to a metabolic state 
8 where time exists as in utero:
9
10 eyes closed, yet fully birthed, 
11 given over to the contractions of days, months, and years, 
12
13 carrying our bodies 
14 in continuum.
15
16 The moths exist in that same abstract as a human held 
17 in their mother’s womb -- 
18
19 impregnated by the desert morning chill
20 neither wholly separate from that cold stone 
21
22 nor wholly together in a union
23 that reduces distinct bodies to a singularity.
