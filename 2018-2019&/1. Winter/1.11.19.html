Mind’s Deep

1 Enter the paradigms of our time,
2 and travel mind’s deep.
3
4 In the ever-expanse and constantly retracting, 
5 a world exists where Christ lives 
6 despite being hammered to a tree.
7
8 I tell you this not because it matters here and now
9 but because hope appears to the astronomical eye.
10
11 Possibilities abound, but the implication of the universal will
12 work out in all the reproductions and interpretations
13 seen by the closed eye while the mind sleeps.
14
15 Being perpetually reborn is a sublime pattern 
16 likely repeating for you and I within an infinite reality.
17
18 I tell you the Word is bound to bring us Love alive
19 in another galaxy when faith is had in what cannot be imagined --
20 that the Father will scatter the ashes of his Son.
21
22 Like seeds when chance outdoes probability,
23 that same you, I, and Him exist again and again. 

1.11.19
