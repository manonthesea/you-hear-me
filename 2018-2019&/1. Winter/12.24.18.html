The Father Knows

1 At the child’s birth, no word is spoken of the sorrow
2 the father knows to come.
3
4 Worldly existence upon a wobbly axis stares
5 up at a carouseling ceiling — 
6 iniquities passed to servant hands,
7 still warm from the womb and already won.
8
9 Paradox is the eyes with which we are born
10 and the means to our peace.
11
12 Joyously, an attendant plants a lily
13 eager for spring bloom — 
14 one hand in the dirt, 
15 plump-worm-filled and carcass-covering.
16
17 The hope for salvation is a bulb sunk into decay
18 where every child born is a potential savior.

12.24.18
