Neighborhood Poet

1 I write poems, written for my loves -- 
2 to be read in new-year winter, southern spring, hot summer, and quick fall
3 and to be read by those who humor me to speak with synecdoche
4 because they know the burning flame-red, soft hair trying my sanity.
5
6 I am a neighborhood poet, who lives and pains 
7 the same insufferable cul-de-sac experience 
8 as the housewife in her brick ranch down the block 
9 clinging to her presupposed, dull belief in a paradox.
10
11 Take these poems; let the word liberate us after the children fall asleep.
12 In that nether, like time’s warm hand upon a woman’s womb 
13 that plants an unspoken thought within a space empty,
14 light shines into darkness turning nothingness into possibility.

1.10.18
