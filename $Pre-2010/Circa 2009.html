Floating Chrysanthemums

1 Japanese Floating Chrysanthemums fall from the sky.
2 Holy Christ, it makes you want to cry out -- where were you?
3
4                 When the red devil,
5                 ablaze in hateful glory,
6                 makes the skies adorn his coronation,
7                 by throwing blooms that dive into the deep blue sea —
8all you can do is pray God is 
9in the heart of men forced to swim,
10in the heart of mothers
11                 not there to see.

Circa 2009
