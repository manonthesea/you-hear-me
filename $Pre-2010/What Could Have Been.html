What Could Have Been

1 Fiameta lives in fog
2 unknowing of what is.
3 Seeming eyes earth-brown 
4 and green like grass,
5 bring her alive within lines.
6
7 Fiameta is and was
8 troubled caused.
9 Forever her existence 
10 commingles with grace
11 on a thread-bound page.
12
13 Fiameta is slow to walk
14 the streets after dark.
15 Hearing baby bawling,
16 lady lying, and homeless dying, 
17 she pretends and portends.
18
19 Fiameta gives faint recognition
20 to brought-down passerby.
21 Yet even when looking into her eyes
22 as they were theirs,
23 they read an unwritten page.
24
25 Fiameta laughs at slightest misconception,
26 seeing life in a different light.
27 Through distant-streetlamp, dim-lit world,
28 and shadow of the real thing,
29 she speaks here and now.
30
31 Fiameta lives in fog
32 unknowing of everything
33 that is and ever was.
34 Fiameta is, but then again,
35 is no more than the page.

Circa 2003
