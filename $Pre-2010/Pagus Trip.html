Paegus Trip

1 Dry fish flake on the riverbed.
2 Summer’s long gone, and still selfish sky
3 refuses to give a-plenty if anything.
4
5 Crossing the creviced bedrock,
6 quiet, dark shadows creep across 
7 the shallows that reap many.
8
9 Up the bank on the other side,
10 watching a column of bodies walk,
11 an internal monologue drifts 
12
13 listening to mouths creak
14 in alien tongue, less language 
15 than sounds with meaning:
16
17 Cannot help but think the Capitol’s racket 
18 fixes vulgar disposition to wit: ransom paid to the right 
19 when one means to say r(e)ich. 
20
21 Before stilled thought wonders afar,
22 should we keep walking with turned backs
23 or turn back and return to the polis?
