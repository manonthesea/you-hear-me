To All My Old Friends

1 The south breeze is blowing.
2 The fire is glowing.
3 I don’t need to tell you
4 everyone misses their old friends.
5
6 As the sweet, familiar voice sings,
7 in the way a true bell rings,
8 I am sitting here missing you,
9 all my old friends.
10
11 The moments are fleeting;
12 the planet is heating.
13 I want to see you again, 
14 all my old friends.

1.31.16
