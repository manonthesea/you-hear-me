The Age of Old Men Loitering

1 A young girl unwittingly knows what men desire.
2 To use it against them, all she does is stand.
3 Firm yet sprite in her control,
4 she holds men like starch in slacks.
5
6 From behind her, mountain-cold water comes 
7 over a supple fall. The force of  gravity that could pull a star in upon itself
8 splashes around to witness her and the old men transfixed by youth 
9 loitering from the burgeoning riverbank of yesteryear.
10
11 Just the thought of the naked, rousing touch 
12 of that cold water shocks the senses; every man is a statue at attention.
13 She lives in the moment standing at the glacial fountain 
14 that comes easy and without anxiousness when days seem to go on without end.
